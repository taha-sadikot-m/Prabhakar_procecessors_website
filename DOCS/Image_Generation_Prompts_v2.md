## VISUAL SYSTEM — SHARED RULES

Every single image on this website must follow these rules without exception:

**Style:** Editorial digital illustration — NOT photography. NOT photorealistic rendering. The look is closer to a high-end Indian lifestyle magazine illustration than a product photograph.

**Background:** Flat warm ivory-cream `#FAF0E6` — perfectly solid, perfectly even. No gradients. No colour shifts. No vignettes. The cream is always the same temperature.

**Exception:** Section 08 (Future/Innovation) images use `#F0F2F8` — a barely-perceptible light indigo-white tint. Still light. Still nearly white.

**Ghost layers (on all cream-background images):**
- Layer 1: Ultra-fine Mughal/Rajasthani palace architectural line-art at **8% opacity**, warm sepia `#8B6914`
- Layer 2: Scattered Indian floral outline motifs — lotus, paisley, leafwork — at **5% opacity**, warm gold `#D4AF37`
- Layer 3: Barely-visible diagonal textile weave grid at **3% opacity**, warm `#F5E6C8`

**Textile colour palette (what appears on fabrics in images):**
- Saffron `#F7941D` · Crimson `#CC2936` · Deep Indigo `#1A237E`
- Fuchsia `#E0457B` · Peacock Teal `#168AAD` · Marigold `#FFB627`
- Zari Gold `#D4AF37` · Ivory Cream `#FAF0E6` · Warm Navy `#20222D`

**Lighting:** Soft directional light from upper-left. Warm. Natural. No harsh highlights.

**No:** Text · Logos · People (except Section 09) · Photography-style realism · Dark backgrounds
