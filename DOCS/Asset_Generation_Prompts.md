## GENERATION TIPS

**For the VIDEO (V-01):** Use Higgsfield's video generation. If the result isn't loopable, generate 2 versions and crossfade between them in post. Alternatively Runway Gen-3 or Kling handle slow-motion fluid well.

**For SWATCH IMAGES (S-01 through S-12):** These are close-up fabric macro shots — image generators handle these extremely well. Generate at the highest available resolution. If the first result isn't quite right, the most important thing is the COLOR and TEXTURE feel — a rough swatch that has the right color and textile feel is more useful than a perfect-looking one in the wrong shade.

**For PROCESS IMAGES (P-01 through P-05):** These don't need to be 100% photorealistic factory photos — even if they look slightly painterly or stylized, they'll work as section backgrounds. The mood and color are more important than perfect realism.

**For BACKGROUND TEXTURES (B-01 and B-02):** These MUST be very low contrast and subtle. If the generator makes them too vivid, you can desaturate and reduce contrast in any photo editing tool before sending.
