1.  Company Overview
Prabhakar Processors Pvt Ltd is a well-established, privately held textile processing company headquartered in Surat, Gujarat, India. The company specialises in the dyeing and printing of a wide range of fabrics — from manmade and synthetic fibres to natural and blended textiles. With over four decades of sustained presence in the textile industry, Prabhakar Processors has built a reputation for delivering consistent quality at competitive prices, making it a preferred processing partner for fabric manufacturers, exporters, and garment houses across India.
The company's core philosophy revolves around three pillars: superior quality, competitive pricing, and on-time delivery. Operating as a one-stop solution for all dyeing and printing needs, it eliminates the complexity of dealing with multiple vendors, offering clients a seamless A-to-Z processing experience under one roof.

6.1  Dyeing Services
▸  Piece Dyeing — Colouring individual lengths of fabric to achieve perfectly uniform shades.
▸  Solid Dyeing — Batch dyeing for consistent, high-volume colour application.
▸  Cationic Dyeing — Advanced technique for synthetic and blended fibres requiring specialised cationic dyes, offering brilliant shades with excellent wash fastness.
6.2  Printing Services
▸  Screen Printing — A classic and highly versatile printing method delivering rich, vibrant designs on fabric through woven mesh screens.
▸  Discharge Printing — A sophisticated technique where a discharge agent bleaches colour from dyed fabric, leaving intricate, soft-hand patterns with a natural feel.
▸  Digital Printing — State-of-the-art digital inkjet printing enabling photographic quality, unlimited colour, and rapid sample development — ideal for fast-fashion and customised orders.
6.3  Finishing Services
▸  Shearing — Precision surface cutting to produce a clean, smooth fabric finish.
▸  Sueding — Mechanical abrasion finishing to impart a suede-like, soft texture to fabric.
▸  Water Repellency Treatment — Application of latest-generation hydrophobic chemicals for moisture-resistant fabric performance, popular in sportswear and outdoor textiles.
▸  Soil & Stain Release Finishing — Chemical finish treatment enabling easy cleaning and maintenance of fabrics.
6.4  Fabric Compatibility
▸  Manmade / Synthetic Fabrics — Polyester, nylon, viscose, acrylic, and their derivatives.
▸  Blended Fabrics — Poly-cotton, poly-viscose, cotton-linen blends, and other fibre combinations.
▸  Natural Fabrics — Cotton, silk, linen, wool, and other natural-origin textiles.
